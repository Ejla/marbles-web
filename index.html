The Friction-Free Entryway

Your mind doesn’t work in neat categories.

Some thoughts are tasks. Some turn into ideas. Some belong in a journal. Some will become a project - but not right now. Most tools force you to decide too early.

What is this? Where does it go? That friction adds up. So things stay in your head instead. They get lost, or pile up and make your mind feel cluttered.

Marbles gives you one place to put everything.

  • Dump your thoughts now
  • Organize when you’re ready

Inbox

- for all the brain-dump moments

When your head feels full, open the inbox and unload it. Capture ideas, reminders, and half-formed thoughts without thinking about structure. It’s a temporary holding space where everything is welcome.

Lists

- for organizing and staying on track

Create lists for groceries, packing, weekend tasks, or long-term goals. Reorder items easily and check things off as you go. Lists are there when you need structure clarity and momentum.

Notebooks

- for things you’re actively working on

Use notebooks for projects, hobbies, and ongoing ideas that deserve more room. They help you gather related notes and lists in one focused space.

Journals

- for reflection and perspective

Keep a record of your days when you feel like it. Look back, notice patterns, and understand yourself a little better over time.

Projects

- when things start to connect

Group lists, notes, and journals together. Keep everything related in one focused space.

Why Choose Marbles?

Everything in one place

Marbles brings notes, lists, and journals together so you don’t have to switch between tools. Everything lives in one place, which makes it easier to stay consistent.

Real Privacy

Your data stays yours. With optional client-side encryption, your thoughts and plans remain private and under your control.

Practical and Mobile Friendly

Quick to use on the go, with layout that stays clear on any screen, adjustable fonts and light and dark mode.

Easy to use from the start

Nothing to figure out upfront. Write first, and let things take shape later.
